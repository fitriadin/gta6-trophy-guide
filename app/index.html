    Roadmap to the platinum

    One playthrough, in this order. Built from the official descriptions: story chapters and missions first, side content as it opens, then the long cleanup counts after the credits.

    ~200 hoursplatinum total
    4 / 10difficulty — long, not hard
    1 playthroughno mission replay, no New Game Plus
    15 + 1 savesmanual slots plus autosave
    0 onlineno difficulty, medal or online trophies

    1. Phase 1 Story, Chapters 1–2

      Play the story. Chapter 1 opens with Gone Fishing and ends with Paradise Lost; Chapter 2 ends with the second chapter trophy and unlocks both playable characters, which opens most side content. Do the Chapter 1 balcony meditation before Chapter 1 ends — it is the one thing on the list that can slip away (see Missable). Keep a save at the start of each chapter.

      Trophies: Just Like Paradise · Paradise Lost · T**s Ass Drug Money · Trust · Break the Bank · Family Matters

    2. Phase 2 Story, Chapters 3–4, plus side strands

      Finish Chapters 3 and 4, picking up stranger strands as they appear. Race Against Yago, the Yago street-racing strand in Chapter 3, is time-sensitive — save first. Near the end of Chapter 4 the optional mission That’s Where The Money Is unlocks the bank vaults. The Chapter 3 mission Yasmani’s Jewelry Jobs hands you the Snap Gun you need for a clean jewelry robbery.

      Trophies: Vice City, Baby! · Top Of The World, Ma · DWNFALL · Hi-Jacked · Snow Storm · Strangers & Freaks (in progress)

    3. Phase 3 Final mission, then the open world

      Complete the final mission for the gold. Keep one untouched save from just before it. After the credits the 100% checklist appears at Progress > Stats > 100% Completion — everything you already finished counts toward it. From here, everything left on the list stays available.

      Trophies: You Gotta Know When to Get Off · A Long Road (in progress)

    4. Phase 4 Side content cleanup

      Finish the remaining stranger finales — Andy, Kenton, Billy, Randy, Tyrone — and Bret’s hunting questline. None of it is missable; the finales stay open after the story ends. Check the Strangers & Freaks progress counter after each strand: Andy, Billy and Tyrone have their own trophies and do not count toward its 8.

      Trophies: Road to Rehabilitation · This Was No Boat Accident · The Lone Rider · Supply & Demand · Spin On This! · Fair Game · Strangers & Freaks

    5. Phase 5 Counts, collectibles, 100%

      Grind the long counts: 250 stunts (300 for 100%), 42 Shooting Range gold medals, 100 WAiNK locations, 100 vehicles discovered, 52 areas explored, 18 meditation spots, 3 card sets, 50 Challenges, 8 garage properties, 10 registered vehicles, $250,000 laundered, $100,000 spent. Then 100% completion — and the platinum.

      Trophies: Cunning Stunts · The Shootist · You WAiNKer! · Whip It Good · The Goodtime State · Positive Mental Attitude · Don’t Touch My Deck · One Last Thing · Little Boxes · On the Spot · Dime on the Dollar · Treat Yo Self · A Long Road · Vice Got a Price!

    Cheats disable trophies and saving — and none of the 61 cheats work before the credits anyway. Play it straight.

    Missable trophies

    Exactly one trophy on this list can be missed. Everything else stays available after the credits, including the side-story finales and the 100% checklist.

    Bronze

    Positive Mental Attitude

    Potentially missable

    Official description: “Meditate at every spot across Leonida.” PSN tracks 18 spots across 10 locations.

    The Chapter 1 balcony meditation — Lucia, at Jason’s house, the morning after Down to Earth — disappears after Chapter 1. Do it during Chapter 1 and keep a Chapter 1 save. Spots show as purple icons when you are close, and each outdoor spot counts twice if both characters meditate there. Whether that one balcony spot is strictly required is still unconfirmed.

    Sources: PSNProfiles; PowerPyx (via GTA Zone); Output Lag; KeenGamer

    Save advice

    There are 15 manual save slots plus 1 autosave, no mission replay and no New Game Plus. Keep one save at the start of each chapter, one from Chapter 1 with the balcony meditation done, and one untouched save from just before the final mission. Save before any time-sensitive strand (Race Against Yago) and before any one-off stunt (the VCIA plane theft, the Rhino tank).
